Reads the actual Pacemaker fencing definitions and runtime state. This does not reboot or power off nodes, change configuration, enable devices, clear failure counts or prove physical fencing.

  • Expected cluster identity, node membership, quorum and node state.
  • Correct STONITH agent, iDRAC address, userid, saved credentials and Pacemaker nodename mapping.
  • Enabled, managed and active devices; global fencing and maintenance settings.
  • Configured monitor operations, timeouts, failure-timeout and recorded failure counts.
  • Fence agents and read-only controller status from surviving peer nodes.

Missing recurring monitors are reported as warnings. Disabled, stopped, failed or blocked devices and non-zero failure counts fail verification. Failure records are left intact for diagnosis.
